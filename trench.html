Setting 1 — Trench

This is a mood board, not a render. These scenes are hand-drawn vector shapes, the same way the current prototype works. Tomorrow Reactor generates the actual video and nobody on the team draws anything. What this shows you is palette, composition, how legible an unrequested object is against the background, and the exact prompt text we would send. Judge those.
Why this setting suits a world model. One lamp cone, suspended silt and near-black water everywhere else. Generated video falls apart on clean detail in good light; it holds up in murk. Warping reads as current, noise reads as silt, colour bleed reads as water.

Why the blind spots stop being a contrivance. Her lamp reaches about four metres. You have the ROV camera behind her and she does not. She cannot see her own visor. She stirs up silt and loses the room for thirty seconds. None of that had to be invented.

The clock. Gas. Every minute spent chasing a shape in the dark is gas she does not get back.
