What this is. A playable prototype of a hackathon project. Wren decides what to say from the state of the scene, and the scene generator sometimes places things in the room that were never requested. An observer step compares what was asked for against what got rendered, and anything unaccounted for is handed to Wren as something she has just seen. She trusts her own eyes over you.
Real here. The anomaly loop, the observer diff, the buffering and pacing, the escalation, her memory of what she has seen.
Stubbed here. The scene is drawn procedurally instead of by a video world model, and her lines come from a local engine instead of a language model. Both are one file each in the full build.
